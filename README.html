XPact Engine

XPact is Simgenics's specialized C++/C# game engine, purpose-built for industrial training and simulation. Simgenics delivers high-fidelity training products to regulated industries — mining, nuclear, power, and adjacent verticals — where deterministic simulation, hot-reloadable gameplay code, and immersive presentation are first-class engineering concerns rather than afterthoughts.

Engineering constants:

  • Languages: C++ for the engine; C# for gameplay and tooling, transpiled to C++ via XIL2CPP.
  • Graphics API: Vulkan-only.
  • XR API: OpenXR-only.

Three-Tier Reusability Model

The repository is organized into three reusability tiers (master-plan Section 2). Each tier follows an Unreal-mirror layout. Lower tiers may depend on higher tiers, never the reverse, and tier membership is enforced by XBT at module-resolution time.

  1. /Engine/ — The XPact engine itself. Universal across every Simgenics training product. Unreal-mirror Source/{Runtime,Editor,Developer,Programs,ThirdParty}, Plugins/, Content/, Config/, Build/, Shaders/. Binaries/ and Intermediate/ gitignored.
  2. /Studio/ — Simgenics-internal shared code, content, and plugins reused across multiple training products. Source/, Plugins/, Content/.
  3. /Projects/<ProductName>/ — Per-product training applications (e.g. Mining-Training, Nuclear-Operator-Cert). Each project's .xproject descriptor declares which Engine and Studio plugins it enables.

Hoisting: When a behavior is used by a second product it is hoisted from /Projects/<A>/ into /Studio/; when something in /Studio/ proves universally applicable it is hoisted into /Engine/. Hoisting is a deliberate design action with cross-system review, not an automatic process.
